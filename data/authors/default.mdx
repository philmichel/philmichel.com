I am SVP of Autonomy & AI at [Motional](https://motional.com). 

Previously, I held roles at [Cruise / GM](https://www.getcruise.com) as Distinguished Engineer, Principal Engineer, and Technical Advisor to the CTO. Before that, I ran robot perception and planning teams at [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com). Earlier in my career, I led machine learning at [Toyota Research Institute](https://www.tri.global) and [Toyota Motor Corporation](https://global.toyota/en) and worked as a senior roboticist at [Google / Alphabet](https://abc.xyz).

In a prior life, I was an equity derivatives trader at [Goldman Sachs](http://www.gs.com) in Tokyo for 7 years, trading volatility both discretionarily and algorithmically.

I hold Ph.D. and M.S. degrees in robotics from Carnegie Mellon's [Robotics Institute](http://www.ri.cmu.edu) and BA & MA degrees in computer science from the [University of Cambridge](http://www.cam.ac.uk).
